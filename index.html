FAQ

Questions?

We're keeping Cloud Photo TV simple from start to finish.

Do my guests need an app?

No. Guests can scan your QR code and upload photos directly from their phone's browser.

Can photos appear on my TV live?

Yes. Photos can appear automatically on the event TV as guests upload them.

Can guests see the photos afterward?

Yes. Guests who request the gallery can receive a link to view and save the event photos.

What kind of events can I use it for?

Birthdays, weddings, graduations, baby showers, anniversaries, sports events, parties, and more.
