FAQ

Questions? We've got you.

Do guests need to download an app?

No. Guests simply scan the event QR code and upload photos directly through their phone's browser.

Do I need a TV to use Captured Moments?

No. A TV is completely optional. You can still use the QR code, collect guest photos, access the event gallery, and let guests download their photos without a TV.

How do photos appear on the TV?

If you choose to use a TV, shared photos are automatically added to the event's live slideshow.

Can guests see the photos after the event?

Yes. Photos are collected into an event gallery, and guests can request the gallery by email.

What's the difference between Captured Moments and Plus?

Captured Moments provides the core experience with standard themes and preset designs. Captured Moments Plus adds more customization, higher capacity, and longer gallery availability.

Can I customize my event?

Yes. Plus includes custom colors, backgrounds, logos or photos, premium themes, and additional event branding options.
